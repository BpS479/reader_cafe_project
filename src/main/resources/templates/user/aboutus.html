Reader Cafe

University of Technology (Yatanarpon CyberCity) — Project Exhibition 2026

A digital sanctuary where books and coffee meet — developed by a team of five passionate students.

Our Story — A Journey of Five

Reader Cafe was born as an academic project for the University of Technology (Yatanarpon CyberCity) Project Exhibition 2024. What started as a simple assignment quickly evolved into a shared passion project for a team of five students who believed in the power of combining literature with technology.


Our team came together with a simple yet ambitious goal: "To create a digital space where people can experience the warmth of a cozy café and the richness of a well-curated library." We wanted to build something that wasn't just functional, but meaningful — a platform that could genuinely bring joy to readers everywhere.


Through countless late-night coding sessions, design debates, and coffee-fueled brainstorming meetings, we transformed our vision into reality. Reader Cafe is the result of our collective effort, creativity, and dedication to making reading more accessible and enjoyable for everyone.


This project is more than just a grade — it's our gift to the world of readers.

Our Philosophy

We believe that reading is not just an activity — it's a transformative experience. Every page turned is a journey into new worlds, and every sip of coffee is a moment of warmth, reflection, and connection. We exist to nurture this beautiful relationship between books and coffee.

Our Community

Reader Cafe is more than a platform — it's a home for thinkers, dreamers, and storytellers from around the world. We foster a welcoming space where readers can connect, share recommendations, participate in book clubs, and grow together as a community of lifelong learners.

Our Mission

To create a cozy digital sanctuary where people can disconnect from the overwhelming digital noise and reconnect with the timeless joy of reading, one cup of coffee at a time. We aim to make reading accessible, enjoyable, and deeply fulfilling for everyone, everywhere.

Our Vision

To build a global community of readers who share stories, ideas, and the simple pleasure of a good book paired with a great brew. We envision a world where everyone has access to a warm reading space — whether physical or digital — regardless of where they are.

Our Purpose & Future Plan

Why We Built This

Reader Cafe was created as a response to the growing need for meaningful digital spaces. In a world filled with endless distractions, we wanted to build a platform that encourages focus, reflection, and genuine connection with stories and ideas.


Our core purpose is to make reading a lifestyle — not just a hobby. We want to inspire a new generation of readers who find joy in the written word and comfort in the ritual of enjoying a warm beverage alongside a captivating book.

Future Roadmap

Phase 1 (2025-2026): Launch the core platform with a curated library of 10,000+ books, audiobooks, and interactive reading features. Establish our community through book clubs and author events.


Phase 2 (2026-2027): Expand to mobile apps (iOS & Android), introduce AI-powered book recommendations, and launch our signature "Coffee & Books" subscription box service.


Phase 3 (2028 & beyond): Build physical Reader Cafe spaces in major cities worldwide, create our own publishing imprint, and develop a loyalty program that rewards reading and community engagement.

Our Impact Goal

By 2030, we aim to have 1 million active readers in our community, 500+ book clubs running simultaneously, and 100 physical Reader Cafe locations across 20 countries.


We also plan to launch "Read for Good" — a program where every book read on our platform contributes to providing books and education resources to underserved communities around the world.
